This team requires spec-kit for every rule change and every new feature. Start with /prd-gate (classification, PRD, TRD). Then you must run, in this order, /speckit-specify, /speckit-plan and /speckit-tasks (/speckit-clarify too if questions remain), and implement the tasks of `specs/<feature>/tasks.md` test first, with one commit per task, following prd-gate's execution rules. Do not skip these steps even when the prd-gate plan seems to cover them: review rejects a rule change or feature without `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `tasks.md` under `specs/`. Bugs and refactors go through /prd-gate only.
